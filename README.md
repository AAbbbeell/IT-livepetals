# Taste of Nigeria - Food Blog

A modern, responsive website for an authentic Nigerian food blog and restaurant that sells traditional Nigerian dishes.

## Features

- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices
- **Navigation Bar**: Sticky header with smooth scrolling navigation
- **Hero Section**: Eye-catching banner with call-to-action
- **Signature Dishes**: Beautiful grid layout showcasing 6 popular Nigerian dishes:
  - Jollof Rice
  - Peppered Goat Meat
  - Egusi Soup
  - Amala & Ewedu
  - Suya (Meat Skewers)
  - Moi Moi

- **About Section**: Information about the business with statistics
- **Contact Section**: Contact form and business information
- **Footer**: Links and social media integration

## Prices

All prices are in Nigerian Naira (₦):
- Jollof Rice: ₦2,500
- Peppered Goat Meat: ₦4,000
- Egusi Soup: ₦3,500
- Amala & Ewedu: ₦3,000
- Suya: ₦2,200
- Moi Moi: ₦1,800

## How to Use

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Navigate through different sections using the sticky menu
3. View all available dishes and their prices
4. Contact the business through the contact form
5. Follow on social media through the footer links
